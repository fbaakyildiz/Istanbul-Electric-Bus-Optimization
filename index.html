Transportation simulation

Route and fleet selection for Istanbul electric bus deployment.

This senior design project evaluates candidate Istanbul bus routes with a SimPy discrete-event simulation, comparing electric bus alternatives against a diesel baseline under passenger, slope, distance, charging, traffic, and seasonal constraints.

Open report Read design
SimPydiscrete-event model
MethodRoute-level simulation, replications, confidence intervals, staged ranking
DomainIstanbul public transportation, battery-electric buses, depot charging
OutputRoute feasibility, fleet comparison, emissions reduction, deployment stages
ScenarioBYD K8, Karsan e-ATAK, diesel baseline, summer and winter operation

Method

The model evaluates electrification as an operational feasibility problem, not only an emissions target.

01

Prepare routes

Build candidate route inputs from stop order, distance, slope, schedule, and passenger data.

02

Simulate service

Run route and vehicle scenarios with passenger load, energy use, battery state, and charging behavior.

03

Compare vehicles

Evaluate BYD K8 and Karsan e-ATAK against the diesel baseline in seasonal operating conditions.

04

Rank routes

Recommend staged electrification using feasibility, battery, charging, passenger, and CO2 metrics.
