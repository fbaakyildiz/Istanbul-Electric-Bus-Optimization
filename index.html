Istanbul Electric Bus Optimization

A senior design project using SimPy discrete-event simulation to evaluate route suitability, bus alternatives, energy use, battery behavior, and emissions for staged electric bus deployment in Istanbul.

Open final report Open repo Design document

Pilot focus

Historical Peninsula routes connected to Topkapi Garage and Eminonu-oriented service patterns.

Models comparedBYD K8, Karsan e-ATAK, diesel Karsan ATAK baseline
MethodSimPy discrete-event simulation with replications and confidence intervals
Critical scenarioWinter operation due to HVAC energy demand

Recommended Route Staging

First stage

93, 33, 36KE, 35

Second stage

336E, 38E, 33B

Workflow

Route, stop, schedule, and passenger data
  -> Distance and slope preparation
  -> Vehicle and season scenario setup
  -> SimPy discrete-event simulation
  -> Replication analysis and confidence intervals
  -> Route feasibility and emissions ranking

Decision Criteria

  • Scheduled service completion
  • Battery state of charge and depot charging visits
  • Passenger fulfillment and vehicle capacity
  • Total energy consumption and CO2 emissions
  • Seasonal robustness, especially winter operation
